Privacy policy · last updated 30 September 2026

Keystone is a puzzle game by Laurel Games (hi@laurel.games). We do not run a server for Keystone and we do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any account. Your game — the board, your monuments, your best score and your settings — is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. It is not backed up to the cloud.

Consent

On first launch, and whenever you open Settings › Privacy choices, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for (or lets you change) your consent where the law requires it, for example in the EEA, the UK and US states with privacy laws. Advertising and analytics start only after that choice and follow it. The User Messaging Platform stores your choices on your device and passes them to the services below.

Advertising

Keystone shows ads through AppLovin MAX, which works with Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), Unity Ads and ironSource LevelPlay (including the ironSource Ad Quality SDK). These services receive your device's advertising identifier, basic device information and your IP address (from which they infer an approximate location) to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud. Where you have not consented, or have opted out of the sale or sharing of your data, they show non-personalised or limited ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Each provider's policy: AppLovin, Google, Unity, ironSource / LevelPlay.

Analytics and crash reports

With your consent, Keystone uses Google Firebase Analytics to count how the game is played (games started and finished, scores, monuments completed, ads watched, purchases), Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes, and Adjust (with Google Play's Install Referrer library and the Google advertising ID library) to measure where installs come from. These receive app-instance and device identifiers and the events listed above, never anything you type.

Firebase Remote Config, which lets us switch features such as ads on or off remotely, is used whatever you choose; it receives an app-instance identifier and nothing about how you play.

Policies: Google, Adjust.

Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play Billing; we never see your payment details. The game checks your purchase on your device.

Children

Keystone is not directed at children and is intended for adults.

Your choices

Settings › Privacy choices changes your consent at any time and, in US states with privacy laws, is where you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. Sound and haptics settings stay on your device. For questions or requests, write to hi@laurel.games.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's use of data changes, and change the date above.
