Support

Write to hi@laurel.games. It helps to include your phone model and the version number shown at the bottom of Keystone's Settings screen.

I bought Remove ads but still see ads

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in to Google Play with the account you bought it with. Remove ads takes away the banner and the ads between games; the videos you choose to watch for a revive or new pieces stay available.

Where is my progress?

Your board, monuments and best score are stored only on the phone you play on. They are not backed up, so they don't move to a new phone and are lost if you uninstall the game.

How do I change my privacy choices?

Open Settings › Privacy choices. See the privacy policy for what each service receives.

Refunds

Purchases go through Google Play. You can ask for a refund from your Google Play order history.

Sound and vibration

Both can be switched off in Settings. The game also follows your phone's "Remove animations" accessibility setting.
